How it works

No setup and no account required to get started.

  • Save
    link

    Save in seconds

    Paste a URL, share from any app, or just copy a link. Kiplet notices it and offers to save it.

  • Auto
    fill

    Details filled in

    Title, cover, and content type are picked up automatically.

  • Focus
    Chill

    Focus and Chill

    Focus for what you want to learn from, Chill for what you want to enjoy. Switch anytime.

  • S2
    E5

    Track progress

    Set a status and leave notes. Books track pages, series track season and episode.

  • Tap
    Swipe

    Simple gestures

    Tap to open, long press to edit, swipe left to delete.

  • Sync

    On all your devices

    Sign in to sync your list between Android and Windows. Without an account, everything stays on your device.
