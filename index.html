Why LaterBox

Built for people who collect too much and revisit too little.

Most tools stop at saving a link. LaterBox tries to make that link useful again by adding context, structure, and progress.

01

From raw link to usable item

Titles, descriptions, images, source site, and content type are extracted automatically so your list is readable instead of cryptic.

02

Focus and chill are different moods

Articles and books often need a different queue than movies, series, or casual videos. LaterBox treats that as a product concept, not an afterthought.

03

Progress matters

A saved item is only half the story. Progress tracking helps you return to what you started instead of endlessly re-saving new things.

Common Questions

Is this the full source repository?

No. This repository is meant to be a public landing page and release hub. It shows the project clearly while keeping the full production codebase private.

Where do I download the app?

Download links point to GitHub Releases, where Android APK and Windows MSI packages are published.

What makes LaterBox different from bookmarks?

LaterBox parses metadata, infers content type, separates focus from chill consumption, and supports progress tracking for longer-form content.

What is the main technical angle of the project?

Kotlin Multiplatform, Compose Multiplatform, local-first persistence, metadata parsing, and shared domain logic across targets.
